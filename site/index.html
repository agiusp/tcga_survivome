About the data

  • One row per patient, harmonized from the TCGA Clinical Data Resource (Liu et al., Cell 2018), PanCanAtlas supplementary files and cBioPortal. All open access.
  • Therapy fields are sparse: “no record” means nothing was recorded, not that the patient was untreated. Comparing survival by treatment received after diagnosis is also prone to immortal-time bias.
  • Follow-up is short for many cancer types. TCGA-CDR recommends the progression-free interval over overall survival for most of them.
  • Results are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple testing. This is a research tool and not for clinical use.

Kaplan–Meier, log-rank and Cox estimates are computed in your browser and are tested against R’s survival package.
